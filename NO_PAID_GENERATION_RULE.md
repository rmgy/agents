# No Paid Generation Rule

**Budget: ₹0 until you've answered all 7 core questions and validated through prototyping.**

This rule enforces a disciplined ideation-to-production workflow that eliminates wasteful AI generation spending by front-loading strategic thinking and low-cost prototyping.

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Generation Validation (Free)

Before any paid AI generation, you must complete these 7 questions:

### 1. What is the story?
- **One sentence summary** of the core narrative
- **Audience impact**: Why does this matter to your viewer?
- **Hook**: What grabs attention in the first 3 seconds?
- **Payoff**: What's the emotional or practical takeaway?

**Validation:** Can you pitch this in 30 seconds to a peer and get genuine interest?

### 2. What are the shots?
- **Shot list** (minimum 8-12 distinct visuals)
- **Sequence order**: How do they build the story?
- **Duration per shot**: Rough timing
- **Transition logic**: Why does shot B follow shot A?

**Validation:** Can you storyboard this with pen & paper or simple sketches?

### 3. What emotion should each shot create?
- **Emotional intent per shot**: (tension, relief, surprise, joy, intrigue, etc.)
- **Visual mood**: (dark/light, fast/slow, close/wide)
- **Audio pairing**: (music, sound design, silence)
- **Cumulative arc**: How does emotion escalate from shot 1 → final shot?

**Validation:** Does the emotional journey have peaks and valleys, or is it flat?

### 4. What visual assets are required?
- **Existing assets**: Stock footage, music, photos you own
- **Real-world shoots**: Can you film parts yourself? (people, places, objects)
- **Design elements**: Graphics, text, lower-thirds, titles
- **Visual gaps**: What CANNOT be shot or sourced?

**Validation:** Separate "nice to have AI video" from "essential AI video"

### 5. What movement happens?
- **Camera motion**: Pan, zoom, cut, slow-mo, speed-up
- **In-frame motion**: What moves within each shot? (people, objects, text)
- **Transitions**: Cuts, dissolves, wipes, effects
- **Pacing**: Is it kinetic or static? When does movement stop?

**Validation:** Can you simulate movement with existing clips, keyframes, or simple edits?

### 6. Can I prototype the movement without AI video?
- **Using existing footage**: Pull from stock libraries (Unsplash, Pexels, YouTube, your archive)
- **Using graphics**: Motion graphics, animated text, transitions
- **Using simple edits**: Quick cuts, speed adjustments, sound design
- **Rough animatic**: A rough 30-second "sketch" showing shot flow and pacing

**Validation:** Build a 70% prototype. If it works at 70%, it'll work at 100% with AI.

### 7. What exactly needs AI generation?
- **Specific gaps**: 2-3 shots you cannot source or prototype otherwise
- **Duration needed**: (Total minutes of AI video, not full story)
- **Visual spec**: (Resolution, style, realistic/stylized, aspect ratio)
- **Acceptance criteria**: What makes this shot "good enough"?

**Validation:** Can you write a detailed prompt for an AI service? If not, it's not ready.

## Phase 2: 50→10→3→1 Funnel (Zero Budget Until Phase 3)

### Stage 1: 50 Ideas
**Input**: Core topic, audience, goal
**Process**:
- Brain dump: 50 concept variations (5 min per concept max)
- Rapid evaluation: Does it answer the 7 questions? (Yes/No/Partial)
- Keep: 20-30 ideas that score 6+ on "story clarity"

**Output**: 50 one-liners, ranked by immediate appeal

---

### Stage 2: 10 Rough Prototypes
**Input**: 10 ideas from Stage 1
**Process**:
- Complete all 7 questions for each
- Build shot list (text only, no visuals)
- Create simple storyboard (hand sketch or AI-free design tool)
- Source 50-70% of assets from free/existing libraries
- Assemble rough animatic in your video editor (Adobe, DaVinci, CapCut)
- **No AI video generation yet**

**Output**: 10 rough cuts (30-60 sec each), each scoring 7+/10 on "story works without fancy visuals"

**Time investment**: 30-45 min per concept

---

### Stage 3: 3 Strong Concepts
**Input**: 10 rough prototypes + feedback
**Process**:
- Pick 3 strongest based on:
  - Emotional impact (does rough cut still land?)
  - Prototype quality (did your 70% version work?)
  - Clarity of AI needs (do you know exactly what to generate?)
- Refine each concept's visual direction
- Write detailed AI generation briefs (1 paragraph per shot needing AI)
- Create detailed mood boards (reference images, color, style)
- Validate movement planning (can you show motion with graphics/existing clips?)

**Output**: 3 polished concepts, each with:
- Final shot list
- Detailed mood board
- AI generation brief (if needed)
- Estimated AI video duration
- Asset checklist (what exists, what to generate)

**Time investment**: 2-4 hours per concept

---

### Stage 4: 1 Expensive Production
**Input**: 1 concept from Stage 3 (winner)
**Process**:
- **NOW budget**: Allocate for AI generation
- Generate only the 2-3 shots you identified in Q7
- Integrate AI video into your full cut
- Polish: Color grade, sound design, final adjustments
- Test with sample audience (5-10 people)
- Iterate if needed (second round of AI generation if strategic)

**Output**: 1 finished production piece

**Budget allocation**: ₹X (only after passing all 7 questions + Stage 3 validation)

## Workflow: How to Use This Rule

### Decision Tree
```
New idea arrives
  ↓
Can you answer all 7 questions? → No → Brainstorm, sketch, source assets
                                 ↓
                            Try to answer each Q
                                 ↓
                            Can you now? → No → Refine concept
                                        → Yes → Proceed to Phase 2
  ↓ → Yes
  ↓
Build 70% prototype (question 6)
  ↓
Does it work? → No → Go back to Q1-3, rethink story/shots
             → Yes → Proceed to funnel (50→10→3→1)
  ↓
Build 10 rough cuts
  ↓
Pick 3 winners
  ↓
Write AI generation brief
  ↓
**BUDGET CHECKPOINT** → Approve spend?
  ↓ → Yes
  ↓
Generate, integrate, polish
  ↓
Done
```

---

## Rules & Constraints

- **No AI generation spending** until Q7 is answered with precision
- **Prototype first**: If your rough cut doesn't work with stock footage, AI won't save it
- **Movement testing**: Simulate motion before paying for AI video
- **Asset audit**: Know what exists before requesting AI fills
- **One-sentence rule**: If you can't explain it in one sentence, it's not ready
- **Emotional arc check**: Every cut should move the emotional needle or reveal new info
- **Movement is a feature, not filler**: Only justify motion if it serves the story

## Tools for Each Phase

### Q1-Q7 Validation (Free)
- Notepad or Markdown file
- Pen & paper for sketches
- Voice recorder (for pitching ideas)

### Prototyping (Free/Cheap)
- Video editor: CapCut (free), DaVinci Resolve (free), Adobe (paid)
- Asset sources: Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, YouTube (royalty-free)
- Storyboard tool: Figma (free tier), Paper (pen & paper), Adobe XD (free trial)
- Stock music: YouTube Audio Library, Epidemic Sound (free tier)

### AI Generation (Paid)
- Runway ML, Loom (video AI)
- Midjourney, DALL-E (image AI if needed for specific shots)
- Synthesia, HeyGen (avatar video if needed)

## Success Metrics

- **Idea-to-concept speed**: 50→10→3→1 in 1-2 days (not weeks)
- **Prototype quality**: 70% rough cut that works without AI
- **Budget efficiency**: Only 20-30% of total production budget spent on AI (rest on creative planning)
- **Approval rate**: Stage 3 concepts get greenlit (2 out of 3 approved by stakeholders)
- **Rework rate**: <10% of AI-generated footage needs regeneration

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

❌ **"Let's just generate it and see"** → Generates 5 videos for 1 idea, wastes budget
❌ **Skipping Q2-Q5** → Results in confused AI briefs, poor output
❌ **No rough prototype** → Can't validate story until you spend money
❌ **Vague AI brief** → "Make a cool cinematic shot" ≠ actionable prompt
❌ **Treating AI as problem-solver** → AI can't save weak story (yet)
❌ **Funding all 50 ideas equally** → Concentrate budget on 3 best only

---

## Checklist: Before You Spend ₹1

- [ ] Q1: Story is one sentence, compelling
- [ ] Q2: Shot list has 8+ distinct visuals, sequenced
- [ ] Q3: Emotional intent defined per shot
- [ ] Q4: Visual asset gaps identified (not "AI will fix it")
- [ ] Q5: Movement plan exists (camera + in-frame + transitions)
- [ ] Q6: 70% prototype built with existing/stock assets
- [ ] Q7: AI generation brief written (specific shots, duration, style)
- [ ] Rough cut tested with 1-2 people (informal feedback)
- [ ] Budget approved by stakeholder/self
- [ ] Backup plan if AI output is bad (can you reshoot or use alternative?)

✅ All checked? **You're cleared for AI generation spend.**

## Example: Applying the Rule

**Idea**: "30-second explainer video about personal finance app"

**Q1 - Story**: "Person realizes they're overspending on subscriptions, uses app to cut costs by 50%, feels in control"

**Q2 - Shots**:
1. Notification sound (phone buzzing)
2. Close-up: Subscription list
3. Animated number: $500/month
4. Person panicking
5. Download app
6. Dashboard interface
7. Finding duplicate subscriptions
8. Canceling services
9. Savings number: $250/month
10. Person smiling, relieved

**Q3 - Emotions**: Tension (0-5s) → Confusion (5-10s) → Solution (10-20s) → Relief (20-30s)

**Q4 - Assets**:
- Existing: App interface screenshots
- Need to shoot: Person reaction shots (30s to film on phone)
- Design: Animated numbers, app UI graphics (use design tool)
- Gap: "Person's hand scrolling through app" ← Candidate for AI

**Q5 - Movement**:
- Cuts every 1-2 seconds (snappy pacing)
- Zoom on subscription list
- Slide transitions for menu items
- Slow-mo on relief reaction

**Q6 - Prototype**:
- Shoot person reaction on phone camera
- Screenshot app interface
- Use animation tool to mock number animations
- Assemble in CapCut with music
- Result: 70% rough cut (missing only the AI "hand scrolling")

**Q7 - AI Generation Needed**:
- **Specific shot**: Person's hand scrolling through app dashboard (3 seconds)
- **Duration**: 3 seconds
- **Style**: Realistic, over-shoulder view, morning lighting
- **Prompt**: "Over-shoulder shot of hand swiping through a financial dashboard on iPhone, morning sunlight, realistic, 3 seconds"

**Budget**: ₹X for 3-second AI video generation
**Result**: 1 polished 30-second explainer, 90% original/sourced content, 10% AI-generated
