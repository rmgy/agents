# WARRIORWAYS — THE STORM
## Complete Production Script: VR + Viral Build
### "The Question Is Not If The Storm Comes. It's Whether You're Still Standing When It Passes."
**Exercise:** Burpee | **Duration:** 12 Reps (~1:55) | **Format:** 9:16 Vertical
**Series:** STORM | **Doodle System:** Reactive — fires per rep + accumulating lightning
**VR Depth:** 3-layer parallax (dark storm-sky background / athlete / HUD storm-system doodle)

---

## VISUAL SYSTEM

```
BACKGROUND:    Dark storm sky — rolling black-purple clouds, no horizon visible
               Background moves CHAOTICALLY — direction shifts with each burpee phase:
                 On DESCENT: sky PLUNGES downward — the ground pulls everything down
                 On PUSH-UP: sky SLAMS in — one compressed beat of depth
                 On JUMP: sky BREAKS upward — the altitude opens for one beat
               The background is NEVER still. It's always mid-catch, mid-shift.
               Combined: FORGE + ROOT + ASCENT parallax vocabulary in one exercise.
ATHLETE:       Midground — explosive, chaotic. Lit by lightning on each rep's jump.
               Between reps: dark, barely visible. In motion: illuminated white-blue.
               The athlete is only clearly seen at their most vulnerable — in the air.
DOODLE HUD:    Foreground — chalk storm system (sparse drops → weather radar → full storm)
DEPTH EFFECT:  3 layers. Background chaos creates violent depth — the storm is present.
               Rep 9 lightning spark enters ATHLETE LAYER — the storm physically touches them.
ACCENT COLOR:  ASH GREY → STORM PURPLE → ELECTRIC BLUE → LIGHTNING WHITE → BONE WHITE
```

## DOODLE ASSET MAP

```
STORM SYSTEM:   Upper frame, FULL WIDTH — unique among all series entries.
                No corner placement. The storm spans edge to edge.
                Starts: 3 sparse rain drops. Grows into weather-radar concentric rings by rep 9.
                Five concentric chalk rings build one-per-two-reps — radar filling in.

LIGHTNING:      One chalk bolt per rep, placed semi-randomly (not lined, not symmetric).
                ACCUMULATE — they stay on screen. The sky fills with bolts through the video.
                Rep 1–5:  Small to medium, jagged, scattered. White-blue.
                Rep 6:    DOUBLE STRIKE — two bolts converge at center-top simultaneously.
                Rep 7–9:  Large, diagonal, crossing. Rep 9: TRIPLE STRIKE simultaneous.
                Rep 10–11: GOLD shift — bolts warm from white-blue to gold. The storm aging.
                Rep 12:   MEGA BOLT — full frame, top-to-bottom diagonal. Holds 0.5s.
                After rep 12: all 12 bolts illuminate simultaneously — the sky filled.
                Then: fade to faint chalk outlines — the storm's record.

TALLY:          Bottom left. Military slash marks — chalk. One slash per rep.
                Groups of 5 with diagonal slash through. The only clean order in the chaos.
                This is not a rep counter. It's a survival record.

WIND LINES:     Behind the athlete (midground layer — not foreground).
                Start: 3 sparse horizontal streaks.
                Build: +3 per 2 reps. 30 lines by rep 9. Fully diagonal. The frame has weather.
                Rep 10+: begin flattening toward horizontal — the storm passing.

EYE MARKER:     REP 6 MILESTONE. A chalk circle forms at center-top: "EYE" inside.
                EVERYTHING in the doodle system holds for 1.0 full second — rain still,
                wind still, lightning static. The eye of the storm. One moment of nothing.
                Then: the chalk circle CRACKS outward. Everything resumes — faster.
```

## VR DEPTH LAYER SYSTEM

```
LAYER 1 — BACKGROUND (storm sky):
  Visual:   Dark rolling storm clouds, black-purple, no visible horizon
  Motion:   CHAOS-BASED — shifts direction with each burpee phase:
              DESCENT phase:  background PLUNGES downward (ROOT signature)
              PUSH-UP phase:  background SHOVES inward (FORGE signature)
              JUMP phase:     background BREAKS upward (ASCENT signature)
  Changes:  Gets progressively more lit as lightning accumulates
            Post-rep 10: starts settling — horizontal motion, slower shifts
  VR Feel:  The storm is responsive — it reacts to the athlete's movement.
            Unlike all previous entries: the background has no single direction.
            It has three.

LAYER 2 — MIDGROUND (athlete + wind lines):
  Visual:   Athlete in full burpee motion. Wind lines behind them (this layer).
  Motion:   Explosive. Every rep direction: down, push, up.
            Illuminated by lightning on each jump — white-blue flash.
  Changes:  Darker at start. More lit as storm reaches peak.

LAYER 3 — FOREGROUND (doodle HUD):
  Visual:   Rain drops, lightning bolts, storm system, tally, wind lines (foreground)
  Motion:   Per-rep reactive. Lightning fires at each jump peak.
  Changes:  Accumulates throughout. The frame builds a record of every rep.
  VR Feel:  Rep 9 spark enters athlete layer — the storm physically crosses into them.

PARALLAX TRIGGER MOMENTS:
  Rep 6 (eye):  THREE rapid shifts — DOWN, IN, UP — 0.6s each. Storm's full signature.
  Rep 9:        THREE rapid shifts again — DOWN, IN, UP — same signature, more violent.
  Rep 12:       THREE final shifts — DOWN, IN, UP — SLOW. Deliberate. The last gesture.

THE STORM vs ALL PREVIOUS ENTRIES:
  FORGE:   Background pushes IN. One direction.
  ROOT:    Background pushes DOWN. One direction.
  ASCENT:  Background pushes UP. One direction.
  HOLD:    Background BREATHES. Time-based.
  STORM:   Background shifts ALL THREE DIRECTIONS per rep. Chaos.
  Together: Five completed entries. Five dimensions. One remaining.
```
