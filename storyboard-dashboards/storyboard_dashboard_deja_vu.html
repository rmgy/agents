Image 9: The Dual Process

Two simultaneous neurological pathways shown—one explicit (conscious memory systems lighting up in silver), one implicit (unconscious familiarity systems in plum). They activate in different order, creating the déjà vu sensation. Storybook illustration of neural complexity.

Image 10: False Retrieval Cue

Close-up of a synapse making a mistaken connection—two neurons' ends almost touching but connecting across a slightly wrong gap. Represents the neural error that creates déjà vu. Silver electrical energy at the connection point. Storybook medical illustration.

Image 12: The Prediction Engine
